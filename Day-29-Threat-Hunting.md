Day 29 - Threat Hunting
What It Is

Threat Hunting is the proactive process of searching through systems, networks, endpoints, and logs to find signs of malicious activity that automated security tools may have missed. Unlike traditional security monitoring, which waits for an alert to be triggered, threat hunting assumes an attacker may already be present and actively looks for evidence of compromise before significant damage occurs.

A threat hunter does not simply respond to alerts. They investigate unusual behaviors, suspicious patterns, and hidden indicators of compromise that may not match existing detection rules.
